Privacy notice

What we collect, and why

  • Without an account: nothing. Your heroes are saved only in your browser or in the desktop app on your computer.
  • With an account: your email address and password (stored hashed), the player name you choose, your heroes, earlier versions of them (hero history, the latest 60 per hero) and the parties you join. We need these to give you the service you asked for (legal basis: contract).
  • Donations: if you buy us a coffee, the payment service behind the link collects your payment details. We never see your card number; we receive what that service shows to the recipient of a donation (usually your name, email address and the amount).
  • Technical logs: our providers keep short-lived logs (IP address, time, request) to run and protect the service (legal basis: legitimate interest).

No advertising, no tracking, no analytics, no selling of data. The app stores its own data in your browser's local storage only to work.
