The free tome

You can use Grimoire of Heroes for free, without an account, with as many heroes as you like on your own device. With a free account, one hero is kept in the cloud and the rest stay on the device.

Grimoire+

  • Grimoire+ adds cloud sync of every hero, a history of each hero, parties, the printable sheet and themes. It is sold as a monthly or yearly subscription, or as a one-off Founder plan while places last.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe and sold through Link, who act as the seller (merchant of record), charge the applicable VAT and send your receipts.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel. You can cancel any time from the account page (Manage subscription) or from your Link account; Grimoire+ stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
  • The Founder plan is a single payment that gives Grimoire+ for as long as the service is offered. If the service ever closes, you keep the app and your heroes on your devices, and you can export them.

Privacy notice

What we collect, and why

  • Without an account: nothing. Your heroes are saved only in your browser or in the desktop app on your computer.
  • With an account: your email address and password (stored hashed), the player name you choose, your heroes, earlier versions of them (Grimoire+ history, the latest 60 per hero) and the parties you join. We need these to give you the service you asked for (legal basis: contract).
  • Payments: Stripe and Link collect your payment details and billing address. We never see your card number; we receive your plan, its status and renewal date, and Stripe's customer reference.
  • Technical logs: our providers keep short-lived logs (IP address, time, request) to run and protect the service (legal basis: legitimate interest).

No advertising, no tracking, no analytics, no selling of data. The app stores its own data in your browser's local storage only to work.
